## Part 4: Fractal Dimensions (Problem Solving)

17. **Cantor Set:**
    *   Start with a line of length 1.
    *   Remove the middle third.
    *   You are left with **2** pieces ($n=2$).
    *   Each piece is **1/3** the size of the original ($s=1/3$).
    *   Calculate the dimension $D$. ($D = \frac{\log n}{\log (1/s)}$)

18. **Menger Sponge (3D Fractal):**
    *   Start with a cube.
    *   Split it into $3 \times 3 \times 3 = 27$ smaller cubes.
    *   Remove the center cube from each face and the very center cube (remove 7 total).
    *   You are left with **20** pieces ($n=20$).
    *   The magnification factor is **3** ($r=3$).
    *   Calculate $D$ (Round to 2 decimal places).
